HHQ Drive Archive Privacy Policy

HHQ Drive Archive is a private, single-user tool operated by the Google account owner for the owner’s own data.

Google Drive access is used only to upload, verify, retrieve, and manage archive files requested by the owner. Google user data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or provided to third parties.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on systems controlled by the owner and are used only to provide the requested archive functionality.

The owner may revoke access at any time through the Google Account security settings.
